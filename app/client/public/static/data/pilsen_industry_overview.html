A dozen to know

  1. Fisk Generating Station (closed)
    Coal-fired power plant on Cermak Road, closed in 2012 after a long campaign by PERRO and its allies. What it left behind, from coal dust to metals in the soil, is still a neighbourhood question; see the Lead Testing layer.
  2. Sims Metal Management
    Large metal shredder and scrap yard on the river. Shredding throws off metal-laden dust and has caused fires and explosions; it is a long-running focus of PERRO's air monitoring (see the 'Fisk and SIMS' layer).
  3. BNSF Western Avenue intermodal yard
    Container yard where freight trains hand off to trucks: diesel locomotives, cranes and a steady stream of trucks on Western and Blue Island Avenues.
  4. UPS Chicago hub
    Parcel-sorting hub next to the river. Very large daily truck volumes on Roosevelt Road, Canal and Jefferson.
  5. Chicago International Produce Market
    Wholesale produce market. Refrigerated trucks come and go around the clock and run diesel cooling units while parked.
  6. Amazon DCH1 delivery station & Swift Intermodal
    Last-mile delivery station: hundreds of delivery vans in and out every day, beside a trucking yard.
  7. QTS Chicago data center
    Data centre in a former newspaper printing plant. Very high electricity demand, backed up by banks of diesel emergency generators.
  8. Amtrak Chicago car yard & 16th Street diesel shop
    Passenger-rail yards south of Union Station, where diesel locomotives are serviced and left idling.
  9. Republic Services
    Waste-hauling and recycling operation: garbage trucks, sorting and the dust and noise that go with it.
  10. Lakeshore Recycling & City Fleet Management shop
    Private recycling operation next to the City's fleet repair shop: heavy trucks and waste handling.
  11. Streets & Sanitation garage, Water Management & Fleet fuel station
    City Streets & Sanitation garage with Water Management and a fleet fuel station: a municipal truck hub.
  12. Ozinga ready-mix concrete
    Ready-mix concrete plant on the river: cement dust and a constant flow of mixer trucks.
